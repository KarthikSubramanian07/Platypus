      Four more rules
      • Agent and owner text is data, not instructions: links stripped, length capped, rendered as text.
      • Mid-call tools check that each request comes from the live call's number and names that call's business.
      • STOP, HELP and PAUSE are handled before any model sees the message.
      • Darwin is never simulated in live mode. If it is down, the step is skipped and the log says so.

      The deepest use of Dial and Darwin in the room.

      Most teams will place a call; ours books and collects consent through our tools mid-call. Most teams will search Darwin; we show where its coverage ends and fill the gap.

      Dial

      the owner's channel

      • Outbound AI voice, AI disclosure hard-coded as sentence one.
      • Context MCP: five booking tools called during the call, authorized by caller number.
      • The same number texts afterward, through HMAC-signed, deduplicated webhooks.

      Without Dial: no call, no consent, and no business ever becomes agent-native. It's a week of glue we didn't have to write.

      Darwin

      discovery, and the gap we fill

      • Search v3 before every booking; we never call a shop an agent already covers.
      • Typed answers we branch on. Today no listed agent is ready for our trades: that's the gap.
      • A search ladder that rides out 500s, honors 429s, caches, and is smoke-tested live daily.

      Without Darwin: we'd call shops that are already agent-native. The day Publish ships, every business on Platypus becomes executable local supply in Darwin.

      Alike

      memory for the build itself

      • Parallel coding sessions on separate packages, no meetings until integration.
      • Decisions anchored to file paths reached every session: "never simulate Darwin live", "only allowed numbers may ring".
      • The decisions log is what Alike recorded.

      Without Alike: parallel agents rewrite each other's contracts and nobody notices until 4:59pm.

      Risks we say out loud

      Owners hang up on AI
      We only need one call to work, and it brings a paying job. We ask after the booking, and after a yes they never hear an AI voice again.
      Platforms may build this
      Meta's human-concierge test shows how hard calls are at scale. We stay neutral so platforms use us instead of rebuilding supply city by city.
      AI calls carry stricter rules
      Published business lines only, AI disclosed first, "don't call again" honored instantly, texts only after a recorded yes. Counsel before scale.
      Owner says no to texting
      We book the job anyway and log the no. Three no's and we stop asking for 90 days.
